Most organisations run an annual cybersecurity training. A short video. A quiz. A certificate. It ends there.

No follow-up. No reminder. Nothing. So when a phishing email lands a month later, the training is long forgotten.

This prototype explores what happens when security doesn't stop at the training. Takes about 3 minutes.

Something feels off? Here's what to do.

1
Pause
Stop before you click, reply or transfer anything. Urgency is a tactic. The pressure is designed to make you act fast.
2
Verify on a different channel
Call the person directly. Use a number you already have, not one in the message. Don't reply to the suspicious email.
3
Report it to your security team
Even if you're not sure. Reporting a near miss is as valuable as catching a real one. It helps protect everyone.
